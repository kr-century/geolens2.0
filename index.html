WHAT THIS IS

GEOLENS is a browser-based GPS camera. It reads your device's most accurate available location continuously and stamps it — live — onto photos and video, with no app install and no backend server; everything runs and stays in this browser tab.

FEATURES

  • Continuous high-accuracy GPS lock with a live accuracy-radius indicator
  • Photo capture with a burned-in geotag (coordinates, altitude, accuracy, time)
  • Video recording with a continuously live-updating geotag overlay
  • Front/back camera switching and optional torch control
  • Metric/imperial units, adjustable photo quality, rule-of-thirds grid
  • One-tap coordinate copy and direct hand-off to Google Maps
  • Share or download captured files — nothing is uploaded anywhere
  • On-device diagnostics overlay for troubleshooting camera/GPS issues without devtools

ACCURACY NOTE

Location comes from your device's own GNSS/Wi-Fi/cell fusion via the browser's Geolocation API. Reported accuracy typically ranges from a few metres in open sky to tens of metres indoors or near tall buildings — this app displays that real, reported figure rather than a fixed guess.

TROUBLESHOOTING

If the camera won't open on Android even after allowing it for this site, check Settings → Apps → your browser → Permissions → Camera — Android requires that permission separately from the in-page prompt. Tap the diagnostics icon (top right) to see the exact error being reported.
